Kota Green Leather Stone is a premium natural stone known for its elegant green tones and sophisticated leather-textured surface finish. Widely preferred for contemporary flooring, wall cladding, outdoor pathways, landscaping, and architectural applications, this stone delivers a perfect balance of durability and aesthetics.

Sourced and processed with precision, the stone is available in multiple size variants to suit diverse project requirements. Its natural texture and earthy appearance make it ideal for both residential and commercial spaces.

Available Sizes

  • 2 ft × 2 ft
  • 4 ft × 2 ft
  • 5 ft × 2 ft

Thickness

  • Approx. 20 mm

Available Variants

Calibrated

The calibrated variant comes with uniform thickness and a smoother backside finish, making installation easier and ensuring better joint alignment. Recommended for interior flooring and wall applications where precision finishing is important.

Non-Calibrated

The non-calibrated variant retains natural undulations and bends on the backside surface. Due to uneven thickness and natural variation, achieving precise joint alignment can be difficult, especially for interior applications. This variant is more suitable for rustic, exterior, landscaping, and natural-finish projects.

Key Features

  • Premium leather-textured surface finish
  • Natural green stone appearance
  • Durable and long-lasting material
  • Suitable for indoor and outdoor use
  • Available in calibrated and non-calibrated finishes
  • Ideal for flooring, cladding, patios, and landscape designs

Applications

  • Outdoor Pathways

  • Garden Areas
  • Commercial Spaces
  • Elevation Cladding
