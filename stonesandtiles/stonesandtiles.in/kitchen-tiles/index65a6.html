ABOUT KITCHEN TILES

What Are Kitchen Tiles?

Kitchen tiles are specially designed ceramic and vitrified surfaces used for kitchen walls and flooring. They are built to resist moisture, heat, stains, oil splashes, and frequent cleaning while enhancing the visual appeal of modern kitchen interiors.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Kitchen Tiles, from durability and maintenance to applications, pricing, and long-term performance.

−

Which tiles are best for kitchen walls?

Glossy ceramic and premium vitrified tiles are ideal for kitchen walls due to easy cleaning, stain resistance, and long lasting finish.

+

Are kitchen tiles heat resistant?

Yes. Premium kitchen tiles are specially designed to withstand cooking heat, steam exposure, and daily long term usage.

+

Which finish is better for kitchen tiles?

Both glossy and matte finishes work well. Glossy offers a premium modern look while matte provides subtle elegance.
